## How to use
1. Filter by `enterprise_domain` or `utility_case_id`.
2. Choose a reference concept and frame it in a fictional electricity, water, gas, or multi-utility context.
3. Develop a distinct, sanitized architecture case study with business capabilities, actors, integration patterns, NFRs, options, and evidence.
4. Label every case **illustrative** until separately validated as publishable. Never claim the case represents actual Utilities delivery without approved supporting evidence.
5. Do not publish client identifiers, internal statuses, timelines, confidential diagrams, contract values, or unapproved cost-savings figures.
